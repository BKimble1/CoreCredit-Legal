4. What CoreCredit does

CoreCredit is a place to record refundable parts-core charges and to keep track of them: what you were billed, which old unit has to go back, which vendor it belongs to, when that vendor's return window closes, whether the unit was returned, and whether the expected credit ever actually arrived.

It works entirely from the figures and details you enter. It does not connect to any vendor's system, does not read your accounts, and knows nothing about your arrangements with a supplier.

Return windows, due dates, aging, totals, and shortfalls are calculated from the values you recorded — including the return window you recorded for each vendor. They are your own working notes, not a statement of any vendor's policy.
