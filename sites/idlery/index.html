What we make.

  • CoreCredit

    Available on the App Store

    Know exactly how much core-credit money is still at risk.

    Repair shops pay a refundable core charge on every remanufactured part, and get it back only by returning the old unit to the right vendor in time — and then actually receiving the credit. CoreCredit is a local-first iPhone ledger that follows every core from the invoice to the credit, so the money that is still outstanding is a number rather than a guess.

    • Built for owner-operated one-to-five-bay auto, diesel, collision, and equipment-repair shops.
    • Returning a core does not settle it — only a recorded vendor credit does.
    • Works offline, with no account and no sign-in.
    • Records stay on the device — there is no Idlery server behind it.

Approach

How we decide what goes in.

Four rules, applied to every screen. They are the reason an Idlery app is small, and the reason it finishes the job it started.

  • One job, done properly

    A narrow tool that finishes its job beats a broad one that almost does. We would rather say no to a feature than blur what an app is for.

  • Local-first where it makes sense

    If an app does not need a server, it should not have one. Fewer moving parts means less to break, less to leak, and nothing to sign into.

  • Honest about what it does

    We describe what our software actually does, including what it does not do. A tool that oversells itself is a tool you cannot plan around.

  • Built for the place it is used

    A parts counter is not a desk. Screens should be readable in a hurry, in bad light, with one hand, by someone who is mid-job.

About

An independent software company with a narrow focus.

Idlery Services LLC is an independent software development company. We create focused tools that make useful work simpler.

Most software for small operators is built the other way round: one large system that promises to run the whole business, and charges accordingly. It usually does ten things adequately and the one thing you actually needed badly — or not at all.

We would rather build the one thing. An Idlery app is scoped to a single job, priced so a small operation can justify it, and designed to be understood in the first few minutes rather than the first few weeks. When a feature does not serve that job, it does not go in.

Everything we ship is built for Apple platforms, works offline wherever it sensibly can, and keeps your data on your device unless there is a real reason for it to be anywhere else. CoreCredit, our first published app, keeps every record on the phone it was entered on.

  • Small shops, real constraints

    The people we build for are working while they use the app — hands busy, gloves on, a customer waiting. That is a design constraint, not a demographic.

  • No account unless it earns one

    A sign-in is a thing that can fail, leak, or lock you out. CoreCredit has none, and nothing else will have one without a reason we can explain.

  • Your data is yours

    Every Idlery app that holds records can hand them back — as a spreadsheet, as a portable file, on demand, with no negotiation.

  • Shipped, or not mentioned

    An app appears on this site the day it reaches the App Store, and not before. Nothing here is a sign-up sheet for something that does not exist yet.
